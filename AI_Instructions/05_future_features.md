## 1. Songs tab — approved and queued
Producers email the admin one mixed track per band; the admin uploads it manually. Track submissions by band and retain the band name, producer, and related participant details for possible future song sync. Accept mp3, wav, m4a, and flac files up to 150 MB, stored locally on disk in `data/songs/`. This is the approved Songs tab, not a separate stems workflow (decision recorded 2026-10-03 in `04_open_questions.md`). Until it lands, producer assigned-band count is only a temporary planned-song count; thereafter show actual song records/counts (`09_producers.md`).

## 4. Printing / display
Do you want band rosters printable, or displayed on a shared screen/projector at the event?
**Both.** (Answered 2026-09-29, homework P-5. Consistent with `06` §I, which answers yes to both printer-friendly rosters and a projector/display mode.)

What "both" means concretely:
- **Print view** — one band per page or a compact all-bands sheet, admin's choice at print time. Includes band name, every member with *all* their primary instruments, producer, shared availability blocks, and any active warnings. Participant emails are included (`06` §I).
- **Projector view** — large-type, read-at-distance layout for the room. Shows band name and member names only. **No emails and no equipment details on the projector** (`06` §F: equipment and sharing details are admin-visible, not public).
- Both views read the same committed band data. Neither can edit.
