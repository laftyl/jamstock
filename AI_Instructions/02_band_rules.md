# 02 — Band Rules

## 1. Band size
What is the min/max number of people per band? (e.g. always exactly 4, or 3-5 range?)

## 2. Required roles/instruments per band
List the roles every band needs, and how many of each (e.g. 1 drummer, 1 bassist, 1-2 guitarists, 1 vocalist).
What happens if a role can't be filled (not enough of that instrument checked in)?

## 3. What if someone plays multiple instruments?
Do they pick one primary role, or can the algorithm assign them flexibly to whichever role is short?

## 4. Skill level balancing
- How is skill level captured on the form? (number scale, categories, years of experience, etc.)
- Should each band have a similar average skill level to other bands, or should each band have a spread of skill levels within it (e.g. one "anchor" experienced player per band)?

## 5. Leftover / unassigned people
If people don't fit neatly into a full band (e.g. 3 drummers and only 1 band needs one), what should happen to the extras? (extra practice band, floaters, wait pool, etc.)

## 6. Band naming
Do bands get auto-generated placeholder names (e.g. "Band 1", "Band 2") or something else?
